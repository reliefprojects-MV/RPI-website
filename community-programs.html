Village Outreach

Community Programmes

Education support, health awareness, girls' empowerment, and family-strengthening work across rural Usilampatti.

Why It Matters

Supporting Children Beyond the Classroom

Many children in rural Usilampatti face barriers that cannot be solved by schooling alone. Poverty, limited health awareness, early marriage, weak academic support at home, and restricted opportunities for girls all affect whether a child can learn and thrive.

RPI's community programmes work directly with villages, families, schools, local leaders, and government departments to address these barriers. The focus is practical and long-term: helping children stay in education, supporting adolescent girls, improving health awareness, and strengthening community protection systems.

The Challenge

Barriers Facing Rural Families

Our programmes respond to the real pressures families face in and around Usilampatti, especially those affecting girls and children from low-income farming communities.

📚

Weak Academic Support

Many parents have limited formal education and children often need extra help to keep up with schoolwork.

👧

Girls at Risk

Girls face pressure to leave school early, marry young, or limit their ambitions because of social expectations.

🏥

Health Awareness Gaps

Families often lack reliable information about nutrition, menstrual hygiene, maternal health, TB, HIV, and preventive care.

🌾

Poverty & Migration

Seasonal work, financial pressure, and lack of local opportunity disrupt children's education and family stability.

🛡️

Child Protection Needs

Vulnerable children and families need support, referral pathways, and trusted local adults who can respond early.

🤝

Limited Access to Services

Many families need help connecting with government schemes, health services, and protection systems.

Community Impact

Long-Term Presence Builds Trust

RPI's greatest strength is its long-term presence in the same villages. Families know the staff, tutors, and community workers. This trust makes it possible to discuss difficult issues such as girls' education, early marriage, health awareness, and child protection in a respectful and practical way.
