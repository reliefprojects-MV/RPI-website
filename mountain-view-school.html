Our School

Education That Changes Futures

Mountain View School was founded in 2015 by Susanna Turcotte to provide quality English-medium education to children from economically disadvantaged villages around Usilampatti. Many of these families come from rural farming backgrounds where access to good schooling has historically been limited.

The school fills a critical gap — giving children not just academic skills, but the confidence, language ability, and life-skills foundation to break cycles of poverty and build independent futures. This is especially important for girls, who face additional barriers including early marriage and restricted mobility.

How We Teach

Our Education Approach

We believe every child deserves an engaging, confidence-building education from the very start — regardless of their family's income or background.

🌱

Strong Early Foundation

Research shows socioeconomic background shapes learning outcomes as early as age six. Our preschool programme uses activity-based techniques inspired by the Institute for the Achievement of Human Potential, giving every child a confident start before gaps can widen.

🧠

Whole Brain Learning

Our teachers receive regular training in Whole Brain Learning techniques — an approach that engages children's full cognitive and emotional capacities. This makes lessons more memorable, inclusive, and effective for children from all backgrounds.

📖

Chrysalis World Curriculum

In primary grades we partner with the Chrysalis World curriculum provider, delivering a structured, enriching programme that develops critical thinking, creativity, and holistic development — going beyond rote learning to prepare children for life.

🗣️

English-Medium Instruction

All teaching is delivered in English, giving children from rural villages the language skills that open doors to higher education, careers, and opportunities far beyond their immediate community.

👧

Child-Centred Environment

The school provides a safe, supportive space where children — particularly girls — can learn without the pressures of early marriage, domestic responsibilities, or social discrimination that affect many peers in the region.

🤝

Community Employment

The school employs over 40 community members as teachers, support staff, drivers, and maintenance personnel — making it a source of stable local livelihoods as well as education.

Students & Support

Enrolment & Fee Support

Mountain View School is committed to making quality English-medium education accessible to children from rural and low-income families. Fees are kept as affordable as possible, while additional support is provided to families facing financial hardship.

Growing With Our Community

After receiving official recognition as a Nursery and Primary School in 2016, enrolment stood at 54 students. Today, the school serves 305 children from LKG to 5th Standard.

This growth reflects the trust placed in the school by local families and the continuing need for affordable, high-quality education within the Usilampatti area.

Fee Support Programmes

We recognise that even modest school fees can place a strain on families with limited or irregular incomes. A combination of government support, partial assistance, and full sponsorship helps children continue their education when their families cannot meet the full cost.
