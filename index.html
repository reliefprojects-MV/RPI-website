Relief Projects India supports children and families in rural Tamil Nadu through Mountain View School and village-based community programmes.

Who We Are

Relief Projects India is a grassroots nonprofit working in and around Usilampatti, Madurai District, Tamil Nadu. Since 2009, we have partnered with rural farming communities to give children — especially girls — the education, health awareness, and family support they need to grow up safely and confidently. Our work spans Mountain View School, free evening tuition centres, and a range of community programmes addressing health, protection, and women's empowerment across the villages we serve.
